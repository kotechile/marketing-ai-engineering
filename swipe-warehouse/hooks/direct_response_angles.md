# Swipe Warehouse: High-Converting Hook Formulas

## Direct-Response Hook Archetypes (Ogilvy & Modern UGC)

### 1. The Contrarian Pattern Interrupt
- *"Stop doing [Common Activity] until you watch this."*
- *"Everything you've been told about [Industry Problem] is backwards."*
- *"Your ChatGPT ideas are completely generic. Here is why."*

### 2. The Direct Call-Out
- *"If you run a [Target Persona] business and your CPA is over $50, this is for you."*
- *"Stop guessing which idea to build."*

### 3. The Specific Proof Metric
- *"How we scored 1,948 startup ideas in 30 seconds."*
- *"We audited 5,000 search terms in 20 seconds before spending a dollar."*

### 4. The Value Flip
- *"Pay retail, not resale."*
- *"Your next product is already sitting in your support tickets."*
